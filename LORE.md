# DINO QUIZ BATTLE — The Lore

> The canon behind every string in the game. The in-game **CODEX** (map top bar → CODEX)
> renders the same material from `AUTHOR.LORE` in `author.js`. If you rename a stage, a boss
> or a world, change it in both places.
>
> **Spoilers throughout** — including exactly how to find the five hidden mechanics.

---

## The premise

A book of five worlds, printed and finished. A small dinosaur — **the Reader** — fights its
way through them by answering questions. Every enemy was written; the Reader was not.

Under the last page, somebody kept writing.

## The two endings

| Where | Title on screen | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Beat **ETERNUS PRIME** (World 5, S25-L5) | **GAME COMPLETE** | The printed book ends. *"You defeated ETERNUS PRIME and saved the realm!"* The score goes on the leaderboard. This is the ending the book was written to have. |
| Write fifty pages at **WORLD END** | **GAME ENDED** · AUTHORED | *"— then it was not unwritten after all."* |
| Tear three pages at **WORLD END** | **GAME ENDED** · UNWRITTEN | *"— the page is blank again. it will keep."* |

Worlds 1-5 *complete*. Everything past the margin is not part of the book, so it does not
complete — it **ends**.

## Figures

**THE READER** — you. A small dinosaur who fights by answering. The only thing in the book
that makes choices the Author did not put on the page.

**THE AUTHOR** — wrote five worlds, had them printed, and could not stop. Kept going past the
last page into the margin — five more worlds, each written with less steadiness than the one
before. Speaks in lower case and dashes, from somewhere under the page. Left seams in
everything, because they could not help it.
> — i wrote cracks into everything. it's the one thing i couldn't stop myself doing.

**DQB3** — the parser; the system voice. Keeps the map, the saves and the questions, and
reports in capitals when something is wrong with them (*MAP DATA INTEGRITY … FAIL*). When
the five seams are found it is DQB3 that notices first — and then the Author corrects it:
*"— the system is me."*

**ETERNUS PRIME** — the printed ending. Written to be the end, and for the book it is.

**THE WITNESS** — WORLD END, level 2. Has seen everything you will do; leaves you exposed
and slow by having already read ahead.

**THE ERASURE** — WORLD END, level 4. Removes you from the record: maximum health, and now
and then a power-up, gone as if never written.

**THE UNWRITTEN** — the last page. Not an enemy: the page the Author never finished, and,
the Author admits, the Author too.
> — its health does not go down. mine never did either.

> — what's under the pressure isn't more enemies. — it's less me.

## WORLD END — THE UNWRITTEN

Fought in **THE WRITING ROOM**: a desk, a candle, a sheet of paper. Its question bank is its
own — fifty hard questions in four movements: *The Arithmetic of Endings · The Physics of the
Last Room · The Logic That Refuses to Close · The Author's Own Vocabulary.*

1. **THE BLANK** — erases one of your blessings.
2. **THE WITNESS** — has seen everything you will do.
3. **THE AUTHOR** — rewrites the scene in its favour, and gives itself more room.
4. **THE ERASURE** — removes you from the record.
5. **🕯️ THE UNWRITTEN — the Unbroken Page.** It does not fight and its health does not go
   down. Every weapon is refused in its own words (*"— the page does not burn."*). A page is
   written when the answer is **true**, comes **before half the time is gone**, and **nothing
   was reached for**. Anything else tears it and the count returns to zero. **Fifty pages:
   AUTHORED. Three tears: UNWRITTEN.** Winning grants the ETERNAL attribute *The Unwritten*.

## The five seams — the hidden mechanics

Five marks in the map's top bar. The Author names them in the gate script:
*"the empty hands. the wrong answers on purpose. the waiting. the page you wouldn't put down.
and the one you won by a thread."*

| Seam | How S1 names it | How to find it | What the Author says |
|---|---|---|---|
| `margin` · THE EMPTY HANDS | the empty hands | Replay a boss (L5) you already cleared in worlds 1-5 and win it **without using a single power-up**. | — empty hands. i saw that. |
| `devotion` · THE WRONG ANSWERS | the wrong answers on purpose | Answer **3 wrong in a row while at 95%+ HP**. | — that wasn't a mistake. |
| `patient` · THE WAITING | the waiting | Let the timer **run out 3 times in one battle**. | — waiting is also an answer. |
| `heldpage` · THE HELD PAGE | the page you wouldn't put down | **Hold the map's top bar** (or the ??? at the end of the map) **for 10 seconds.** The marks brighten while you hold. | — you held on longer than the page expected. |
| `thinline` · THE THREAD | the one you won by a thread | **Win any level at 5% HP or less.** | — by a thread. i saw that. |

**The seams do not exist until World 5 is complete.** Before that, none of them can trigger
and their marks are not drawn on the map — a new player never sees that there is anything to
find. Once the book is finished, start a new run (or use any run) to find them.

## Artifacts

- **THE MARGIN** — the white space past World 5. On the map: *COMING SOON* → *???* (the book
  is finished; the margin is not) → *a gap in the page* → *THE UNWRITTEN*.
- **THE BREAK** — what it looks like when the page admits it is a page: a white seam walks the
  screen, stage names become things never written (THREAD EATER, REDACTION, MARGIN WARDEN…),
  the map falls away, black, a caret. It happens twice; the second time is worse —
  *"— you're not supposed to be able to do that."*
- **THE INK** — the syllabus paste box. It never once checked whether the Reader was holding a
  syllabus. After World 10 it listens. Wrong ink: *"— wrong ink."* Keep trying:
  *"— shorter. honest. like a letter."* The right ink is addressed to someone — *hello*,
  *who are you*, *is anyone there*, *thank you*, *author*…
- **THE PEN** — acts on its own: once you have written twenty pages, it holds one tearing page
  together.
- **THE CANDLE** — the Author's face. It stays lit whether or not you say yes.

## Timeline — how a reader meets it

1. **The printed book.** Worlds 1-5, ETERNUS PRIME, **GAME COMPLETE**, score on the board.
   The run that earned it is quietly kept.
2. **The seed (S0).** Moments after the ending: *"— did it feel finished to you?"* The system
   notices things. Do five of them.
3. **The seams.** The five marks light one by one.
4. **The break and the gate (S1).** Next time the map is on screen: THE BREAK, DQB3 fails an
   integrity check, *"— the system is me."* **YES** writes worlds 6-10 and the kept run walks
   into World 6. **NOT YET** — tap the ??? whenever you're ready.
5. **The margin.** The laws stack. After World 8 (S2) the Author asks whether it's holding.
6. **The hint (S3).** After World 10: *"— find the ink."*
7. **The letter back (S4).** Write to the Author in the syllabus box. THE BREAK again, worse.
   *"— nobody writes to the parser."* **YES** opens WORLD END.
8. **The writing room.** Four obstacles, then the Unbroken Page. **GAME ENDED** — AUTHORED or
   UNWRITTEN.
